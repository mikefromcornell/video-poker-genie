Mastering the Math

Video Poker is a game of skill and mathematics. Unlike slots, your decisions directly impact the return of the game.

Exhaustive Expectation Evaluation

The Genie uses a "Brute Force" solver. When you are dealt 5 cards, there are exactly 32 ways you can hold them. For each of those 32 ways, the solver calculates the outcome of every possible replacement card combination from the 47 cards left in the deck.

Expected Value (EV): The average amount of money you expect to win for a specific hold over the long term.

Example: Holding a Pair of Jacks might have an EV of 0.54, while drawing 5 new cards might have an EV of 0.32. The Genie tells you to hold the Jacks because 0.54 > 0.32.

Optimal Bet Sizing

Always Bet Max (5 Coins)

The Royal Flush payout jumps from 250:1 to 800:1 only when you bet 5 coins. This single bonus accounts for roughly 1.5% of the total return. Never bet fewer than 5 coins.

Bankroll Management

To withstand the high variance of waiting for a Royal Flush, you should have a bankroll of at least 3-5 days worth of play, or roughly 2,000 to 5,000 units.
