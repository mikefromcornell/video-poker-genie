Bankroll Management Rules & Principles

Rule #1: Always Bet Max (5 Coins)

The Royal Flush payout jumps from 250:1 to 800:1 only when you bet 5 coins. This single bonus accounts for roughly 1.36% of the total return (99.54% vs 98.18%). If you cannot afford 5 coins at your current denomination, drop down a denomination (e.g. from $1 to $0.25) so you can bet 5 coins.

Rule #2: The 3-5 Day Bankroll Rule

To withstand the natural volatility of waiting for a Royal Flush (1 in 40,391 hands), you should maintain a bankroll of at least 1,500 to 4,000 max bets for extended play.

Rule #3: Session Stop-Loss vs Overall Bankroll

Never bring your entire bankroll to a single machine session. Divide your bankroll into trip session units (e.g. 200 to 400 bets per session) to prevent tilt and ensure longevity.

Mastering the Mathematics of Video Poker

Video Poker is a game of skill, probability, and rigorous mathematics. Unlike slot machines, your decisions directly control the payout return.

Exhaustive Expectation Evaluation

The Genie uses an exact mathematical solver. When dealt 5 cards, there are exactly 32 ways ($2^5$) you can hold them. For each of those 32 ways, the solver calculates the outcome of every possible replacement card combination from the 47 cards remaining in the deck.

Expected Value (EV): The average amount of money returned for a specific hold over the long term.

Example: Holding a High Pair of Jacks has an EV of 7.68 credits (on a 5-coin bet), while holding an unsuited TJQK has an EV of 3.20. The Genie recommends holding the Jacks because $7.68 > 3.20$.
