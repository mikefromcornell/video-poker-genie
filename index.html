Mastering the Math

Video Poker is one of the few games in the casino where a player can have a mathematical edge. The key is playing the Right Game with the Right Strategy.

Exhaustive Expectation Evaluation

This app uses an exhaustive solver. For every hand dealt, there are 32 possible ways to hold cards (2^5). For each way, the solver calculates every possible outcome from the remaining 47 cards in the deck. It then averages the payout of all outcomes to find the Expected Value (EV).

  • Optimal Hold: The combination with the highest EV.
  • House Edge: The difference between 100% and the game's return.
  • Full Pay: A "9/6" Jacks or Better machine (9 for Full House, 6 for Flush) returns 99.54%.

Optimal Bet Sizing

To win at Video Poker, you must manage your bankroll to survive "swings".

Rule #1: Always Bet Max (5 Coins)

Max betting unlocks the Royal Flush bonus (usually 800:1 vs 250:1). This increases the total return of the game by roughly 1-2%. If you can't afford 5 coins at $1, move to a $0.25 machine.

Bankroll Requirements

For a standard Jacks or Better session:

  • Short session (3 hours): 200 units ($250 for $0.25)
  • Serious play: 2,000 units ($2,500 for $0.25)
